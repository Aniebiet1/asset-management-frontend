Welcome and roles

TecresTrackIT is your organization's register of physical assets: laptops, vehicles, machinery, furniture and anything else you need to account for. It records where each item is, who has it, its condition and value, its maintenance, and whether it was found at the last audit.

Your role decides what you see

Your administrator gives you one of four roles. Menu items and buttons you cannot use are hidden.

Signing in securely

Every account uses two-step sign-in: your password, then a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. This protects your organization even if a password is guessed or leaked. You will need an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.

2.1 Your first sign-in

  1. Open the sign-in page and type your work email. Your company's logo and colors appear once the email is recognised.
  2. Enter the password you were given (or the one you chose when accepting an invitation) and select Sign in.
  3. You are asked to set up two-factor authentication. In your authenticator app, add an account and scan the QR code. If you cannot scan it, type the key shown under Can't scan? Enter this key manually.
  4. Type the 6-digit code from the app and select Verify and continue.
  5. Save the 10 recovery codes in a safe place (a password manager, or printed and locked away). Each can be used once if you lose your phone. Tick the confirmation box and select Continue.
Keep these private

Never share your password, QR code, setup key, recovery codes or a sign-in code with anyone, including colleagues or anyone claiming to be from Tecres. Tecres will never ask for them.

2.2 Everyday sign-in

  1. Enter your email and password and select Sign in.
  2. Type the current 6-digit code from your authenticator app and select Verify. Each code changes every 30 seconds.

Lost your phone? Select Use a recovery code instead and enter one of your saved codes.

You stay signed in while you work, and TecresTrackIT renews your session quietly in the background. Use Sign out from your name in the top-right corner when you finish, especially on shared computers.

2.3 Forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in page select Reset it, enter your work email and submit.
  2. Open the email and follow the link within 60 minutes. Choose a new password. For your safety you are signed out of all devices.

After 5 wrong passwords your account locks for 15 minutes. Wait, or reset your password.

Administrators

First-time workspace setup

The first time your workspace's administrator signs in, a four-step wizard prepares the workspace. It only appears once.

Step 1: Profile

Confirm your organization's name, currency, timezone and date format, then select Continue.

Step 2: Branding

Select Upload logo (PNG or JPG up to 5 MB). Colors are suggested from your logo; adjust the Primary, Accent and optional Sidebar colors if you wish and check the Live preview. Your logo and colors then appear across your workspace, on the sign-in page, in emails and on printed asset labels.

Step 3: Seed data

Add your first location, asset type and department, each with a short code. You can add more later in Settings → Catalog.

Step 4: Finish

Review the summary and finish. You arrive on your dashboard, ready to add assets and invite your team.

Recommended next steps
  1. Complete your catalog: locations, asset types, departments, and (if you use structured tags) subsidiaries and branches (section 13).
  2. Choose your label format (section 12).
  3. Invite your team (section 9).
  4. Register your assets (section 5).

Assets

5.1 Browsing assets

All Assets lists every asset with its tag, type, location, status and custodian. Narrow the list with the search box and the asset type, location and status filters. Select an asset to open it.

5.2 An asset's page

  • Header: name, status, asset tag, make and model, serial number.
  • Cards: location, department, custodian (who has it) and purchase date.
  • Overview: condition, asset type, vendor, warranty expiry and, for roles allowed to see them, purchase price and current value.
  • History: every change and movement. Maintenance: its maintenance jobs.
  • Buttons (depending on your role): Label prints this asset's label, Check Out, Correct tag and Edit.
Staff view

Staff see the same page without purchase price, current value and the action buttons. Money values are never sent to staff accounts at all.

5.3 Registering a new asset

Select New Asset. The form has four tabs; only fields marked * are required.

TabWhat to fill in
GeneralAsset tag details, name, location, department, vendor, custodian, status, condition and description.
SpecificationsBrand, model, serial number and, for IT equipment, MAC and IP address.
Purchase & warrantyPurchase price, current value, salvage value and warranty expiry.
Photo & notesA photo of the item and any notes.

Admins, asset managers

Check-out and return

Checking an asset out records who is responsible for it and when it is due back. Check-Out / Return shows every asset currently out; overdue ones are outlined in red.

Checking an asset out

  1. Type the asset's tag, name or serial number and select Search, or select Scan and point your camera at its label.
  2. Check the asset details that appear and select Check Out.
  3. Choose the Custodian, optionally an Expected return date, record the Condition at check-out and add notes. Select Check Out.

Returning an asset

  1. Find the asset in Active check-outs (or search for it) and select Check In.
  2. Record its condition on return and any notes, then confirm. The asset becomes Available again.

Receipt downloads a PDF receipt of the check-out for your records.

Everyone

Maintenance

Maintenance lists all preventive and corrective work, with priority, status and dates. Overdue jobs show their date in red. Filter by status at the top.

Requesting maintenance (everyone)

  1. Select Schedule maintenance.
  2. Choose the asset, give the job a title, pick the type (preventive, corrective, calibration, inspection or upgrade) and priority, set the scheduled date and optionally the vendor or technician.
  3. Save. The job appears as Scheduled.

Progressing and completing work (admins, asset managers)

Use the pencil icon to update a job as it moves through In Progress, Waiting Parts and Completed (or Cancelled). When completing, record the completion date, cost and parts used.

Asset status follows automatically

Starting work sets the asset to In Maintenance; completing or cancelling it makes the asset Available again, and the dashboard updates to match.

Audits

An audit is a physical count: someone walks the floor, scans each asset, and the results are checked by a second person. The person who verifies an audit can never be the person who carried it out.

A verifier can instead reject a submission with a reason; the auditor then resumes and resubmits. An audit can be cancelled before it is verified.

8.1 Creating and assigning (admins, asset managers)

  1. In Audits, select New Audit.
  2. Name it and choose its scope: all assets, or one location, department or asset type.
  3. Open the audit and assign it to the person who will do the count. They are notified.

8.2 Carrying out an audit (the assigned auditor)

  1. Open the audit from Audits (or the notification) and start it.
  2. Scan each asset's label with Use Camera, or type its tag and select Scan. If you find it somewhere else, choose its actual location; if its condition has changed, choose the new condition.
  3. Watch the counters: Expected, Scanned, Matched, Missing and Discrepant. Each item in the list turns from Pending to its result.
  4. When finished, summarise what you found in the remark box (required) and select Submit for verification.

8.3 Verifying (admins, asset managers)

Open the submitted audit. Review the counts, the auditor's remark and each item, then either add an optional remark and select Verify, or give a reason and select Reject. The History panel records every step and who took it.

Administrators

People

People lists everyone in your organization with their role, employee ID, title, department, status and last sign-in.

Inviting someone

  1. Select Invite Person, enter their work email and choose their role.
  2. They receive an email with a link to accept. They choose their own password and set up two-factor at their first sign-in.
  3. Until they accept, the invitation appears under Pending invitations, where you can resend or revoke it.

Each email address can belong to only one workspace on TecresTrackIT.

Changing a role or removing access

  • Edit changes a person's name, role, title, department or phone.
  • Deactivate removes their access when someone leaves; their history is kept. You cannot deactivate yourself.
  • Give people the lowest role that lets them do their job.

Admins, asset managers

Vendors

Vendors holds the suppliers and service providers you buy from and use for repairs, with contact details. Vendors can then be chosen on assets and maintenance jobs. Everyone can view the list; administrators and asset managers can add and edit vendors.

11
Admins, asset managers

Reports

Reports summarises your asset register and its value, and exports it.

  • Totals: assets valued, total purchase value, total current value and average percentage depreciated.
  • Filter by asset type, location and custodian, and by a date range on either the Purchase date or the Depreciation date (one at a time).
  • Group by asset type, location or depreciation band, and sort by any column, ascending or descending. Reset clears everything.
  • Export downloads exactly what is on screen as CSV, Excel or PDF.

Depreciation is calculated on a straight-line basis from the purchase date.

12
Admins, asset managers

Labels

Printed labels let anyone identify an asset and scan it during check-out and audits.

One-time setup (administrators)

  1. Go to Settings → Labels and choose a format: Barcode + QR, Barcode only or QR only. Select Save format.
  2. Under Print sheet calibration, enter your label paper's measurements (label size, columns, rows, gaps and margins) so printing lines up with pre-cut labels. The preview shows how many labels fit on a page.

Printing labels

  1. Open Labels, find assets with the search and filters, and tick the ones you need.
  2. Select Print N Labels to download a PDF sheet, then print it at 100% scale on your label paper.

To print a single label, use Label on the asset's page.

Help and troubleshooting

SituationWhat to do
"Invalid email or password"Check both and try again. The message is the same for every sign-in problem, for security. After 5 wrong attempts, wait 15 minutes or reset your password.
"Invalid authentication code"Make sure your phone's time is set automatically and use the newest code. A code only works once.
I lost or replaced my phoneSign in with a recovery code, then ask your administrator to help you set up the authenticator again. No recovery codes? Contact your administrator.
I can't see a menu item or buttonYour role doesn't include it. Ask your administrator.
I can't see asset valuesValues are only shown to administrators and asset managers.
My invitation link has expiredAsk your administrator to resend it from People → Pending invitations.
"This organization's access has been suspended"Your administrator should contact Tecres.
Labels print out of linePrint at 100% (not "fit to page") and check the calibration in Settings → Labels.
