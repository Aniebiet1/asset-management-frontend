About the platform console

The platform console is where Tecres runs TecresTrackIT as a service. It is a separate application from the one customers use, with its own sign-in, its own accounts and its own address: /platform.

What you can do here

  • Create a workspace for a new customer, together with its first administrator.
  • See every customer workspace at a glance: users, assets, plan status and risk signals.
  • Adjust a customer's user and asset quotas, suspend or reactivate a workspace, and force a password reset for one of its users.
  • Watch for failed-login spikes and other automatic alerts.
  • Invite other Tecres staff to the console and set their role.

What you cannot do here, by design

  • You cannot sign in as a customer or work inside their workspace. You can see usage figures, user lists and activity, but not their assets or records.
  • You cannot use a Tecres console account to sign in to the customer application, or a customer account to sign in here.

Joining and signing in

2.1 Accepting your invitation

Console accounts are created only by invitation from a platform owner. You receive an email titled "You have been invited to the Tecres platform console". The link in it is valid for 7 days.

  1. Open the link. The page greets you by name and shows the role you are joining as.
  2. Choose a password and type it again to confirm, then select Continue to two-factor setup.

2.2 Setting up two-factor authentication

Before you get into the console, you must connect an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password or similar). This cannot be skipped.

  1. In your authenticator app, add an account and scan the QR code. If you cannot scan, type the key shown under Can't scan? Enter this key manually.
  2. Type the 6-digit code the app shows and select Verify and continue.
  3. You are shown 10 recovery codes. Save them somewhere safe and offline, such as a password manager. Each works once, if you ever lose your phone.
  4. Tick I've saved these recovery codes somewhere safe and select Continue.
Never share or screenshot these

Anyone with your QR code, setup key or recovery codes can generate your sign-in codes. They are blurred in this guide for that reason.

2.3 Signing in

  1. Go to the console sign-in page (/platform/login), enter your Tecres email and password, and select Sign in.
  2. Open your authenticator app and type the current 6-digit code, then select Verify. The countdown shows how long you have before you must start again.

Lost your phone? On the code screen choose Use a recovery code instead and enter one of your saved codes.

Sessions are short on purpose

A console session lasts 15 minutes and is not kept when you reload or close the tab. If you are returned to the sign-in page, simply sign in again. Sign out with the arrow icon next to your name when you finish.

Creating a customer workspace

Customers cannot sign themselves up. A platform owner creates each workspace together with its first administrator, then hands the sign-in details to the customer.

  1. Go to Organizations and select New Workspace.
  2. Enter the Organization name. A Workspace slug (a short, unique, lowercase identifier such as harbor-logistics) is suggested from the name; adjust it if you wish.
  3. Under First admin account, enter the customer administrator's Name and work Email. Each email can belong to only one workspace on the whole platform.
  4. An Initial password is generated for you. Use the button beside it to generate a different one.
  5. Select Create workspace.

The confirmation screen shows the workspace, the admin's email, the initial password and the sign-in address once only. Select Copy all, then Done.

Hand over credentials securely
  • Send the email address and the password through different channels (for example email for the address and sign-in link, a phone call or text for the password).
  • Never paste them into a shared chat, ticket or document.
  • The customer sets up two-factor authentication at their first sign-in, so the password alone never gives access.

Looking after an organization

5.1 The organizations list

Organizations lists every customer workspace with its plan status, users and assets against quota, and when it was last active. Select a row to open it.

5.2 Overview and quotas

The Overview tab shows usage against quota, last activity and whether onboarding is complete. Platform owners can change quotas here:

  1. Under Adjust quotas, enter the new User quota and/or Asset quota.
  2. Select Update quotas. The change is recorded in the platform admin log.

The system raises an alert when a workspace reaches 90% of its asset quota and again at 100%.

5.3 Users and forced password resets

The Users tab lists the organization's people with their role, status, whether two-factor is set up, and when they joined. If a customer tells you an account may be compromised, or a user is locked out and cannot reset their own password:

  1. Select the key icon (Force password reset) on that user's row.
  2. Read the confirmation and select Send reset email.

The user is signed out everywhere immediately and emailed a link to choose a new password. The link lasts 60 minutes.

Verify before you act

Only force a reset when the request comes from the customer's known administrator through a channel you trust. Confirm the person's identity if the request is unexpected.

5.4 Activity

The Activity tab shows the organization's recent activity log: sign-ins (including failed ones, with IP address), changes to assets and people, and workspace events. Use it when investigating a support request or a security concern.

5.5 Suspending and reactivating a workspace

Suspending blocks every user of that organization on their next action, with a message that access has been suspended. No data is deleted.

  1. Open the organization and select Suspend workspace (top right). It takes effect at once; the status badge turns to Suspended.
  2. To restore access, select Reactivate workspace.

Typical reasons: an unpaid or expired plan, a customer's request during a security incident, or the end of a contract. Every suspension and reactivation is logged with your name.

Alerts

The system checks every workspace every 15 minutes and raises alerts automatically. Alerts lists them with severity and the organization concerned.

AlertSeveritySuggested action
Failed login spikeWarningCheck Detected login risk on the dashboard. Contact the customer's administrator; force a password reset if the account may be compromised.
Asset quota nearly reached (90%)WarningDiscuss a plan change with the customer; raise the quota if agreed.
Asset quota exceeded (100%)CriticalRaise the quota or agree next steps with the customer.
Plan expiredCriticalFollow up on renewal; suspend if required by the contract.
No recent activity (30 days)InfoCheck in with the customer; they may need help getting started.

When an alert has been dealt with, select Resolve. Resolved alerts stay in the list, marked Resolved, as a record.

Platform owners

Managing platform admins

Admins (visible to platform owners only) lists everyone at Tecres with console access, their role, whether they have set up two-factor, and pending invitations.

Inviting a colleague

  1. Select Invite admin.
  2. Enter their name and Tecres email address, and choose a role. Give Platform support unless they need to create or suspend workspaces, change quotas, force resets or manage admins.
  3. Select Send invitation. They receive an email valid for 7 days and set their own password and two-factor (section 2).

Changing a role

Use Change role on a colleague's row to move them between Platform owner and Platform support. You cannot change your own role, so there is always an owner who can manage the team.

Least privilege

Keep the number of platform owners small. Review the Admins list whenever someone changes job or leaves Tecres, and downgrade or remove access promptly.

Security responsibilities

Console accounts can see information about every customer, so they are among the most sensitive accounts Tecres has. Every console user agrees to the following:

Practice
✓Use a unique, strong password for the console, stored in a password manager.
✓Keep your authenticator on a phone that has a screen lock; keep recovery codes offline.
✓Never share your account, codes or a signed-in screen with anyone, including colleagues.
✓Hand customer credentials over through two separate channels; never in shared chats or tickets.
✓Confirm who you are talking to before forcing a reset or suspending a workspace.
✓Sign out when you finish, especially on shared or public networks.
✓Report a lost phone, a suspicious email or unexpected console activity to the Tecres security lead at once.

Every action you take in the console (creating, suspending or reactivating a workspace, changing quotas, forcing a reset, inviting or changing an admin, resolving an alert) is recorded against your name.

Troubleshooting

SituationWhat to do
"Invalid email or password"Check the email and password. The message is deliberately the same for every sign-in problem. After 10 quick attempts you must wait 15 minutes before trying again.
"Invalid authentication code"Make sure your phone's clock is set automatically, then use the newest code. A code shown a minute ago will no longer work.
I lost my phoneSign in with a recovery code, then ask a platform owner to help you set up a new authenticator. If you have no recovery codes, contact the Tecres security lead.
I was sent back to the sign-in pageConsole sessions last 15 minutes and do not survive a page reload. Sign in again.
My invitation link does not workLinks expire after 7 days or once used. Ask a platform owner to send a new invitation.
A customer's user is locked outAfter 5 wrong passwords an account locks for 15 minutes. The user can wait, use Forgot password, or you can force a password reset (5.3).
A customer lost their authenticator and recovery codesTheir own administrator should help them first. Escalate to a platform owner if the administrator is the one affected.
The new workspace's admin never received their detailsThe console does not email them; the credentials must be handed over by you (section 4). If they are lost, force a password reset for that admin.
